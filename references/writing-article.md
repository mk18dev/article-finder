Title and Abstract are written after the article is completed (stated on both slides). Findings also needs 3-5 tables and 1-2 figures.

## 1. Title — steps 34-35 (pages 41-42)

1. It should be between 8 and 15 words.
2. It should grab the reader's attention.
3. It should clearly state the main topic addressed in the research.
4. It should be as specific as possible and indicate the scope of the topic.
5. There is no point at the end of the title.
6. It will be written after the article is completed.

Formula (page 42, Persian): main title of a research article = **research design + statistical population + geographic area** (طرح پژوهش + جامعه آماری + حوزه جغرافیایی).

Examples on the slide (Persian):
- کاوش الگوهای خط سیر در اشیا متحرک در شبکه های جاده ای
- رابطه تغذیه نامناسب با موفقیت تکواندوکاران ملی پوش البرزی با استفاده از درخت تصمیم

## 2. Abstract — steps 36-37 (pages 43-44)

1. It should be between 200 and 250 words.
2. It should grab the reader's attention.
3. The reader first reads the abstract and decides whether to read the rest of the article.
4. It serves as a showcase for the article.
5. It begins with a short sentence.
6. It shows the main purpose of the research.
7. It shows the scientific or practical importance of the research.
8. It briefly shows the methodology used in the research.
9. The research findings are summarized.
10. It briefly states the challenges in the research.
11. Finally, it highlights the practical or theoretical implications of the research findings.
12. It is written in a single paragraph.
13. In the abstract, we do not have tables, figures, diagrams, or in-text references.
14. It will be written after the article is completed.

## 3. Key Words — step 38 (page 45)

1. It should be between 6 and 8 words.
2. Keywords act as traffic lights and attract the reader's attention.
3. If the keyword is in English, its first letter should be capitalized.

*(skill note)* The checker counts keywords as comma-separated terms, because multi-word terms like "Data Mining" are normal.

## 5. Introduction — steps 40-43 (pages 47-50)

1. It should be between 500 and 700 words.
2. Writing an introduction to an article is one of the most difficult parts of an article.
3. The author must state the general topic of the research.
4. You must answer the question of what is the benefit of reading this article.
5. Which are the most important previous studies that are related to the current research?
6. The main problem and question of the research must be stated.
7. The specific objectives of the research must be clearly stated.
8. The background in which we conducted the research must be stated.
9. The unit of analysis of this research must be clearly stated.
10. The challenge and solution to the problem must be clearly stated.
11. Finally, the author provides a general outline of the structure of the other parts of the article.

Page 49:
12. Write the opening paragraph in simple, non-technical language.
13. The reader should be guided step-by-step to the formal statement of the research problem.
14. Use an attractive opening sentence.
15. Summarize the research done for the last three to five years in this section.

Page 50:
16. Statement of the problem challenge.
17. Recent research should be directly related to your problem.
18. The main purpose of the research should be stated in the form of a short problem statement or the form of the main research question.
19. The research objectives should be written from the most general to the most specific.

## 6. Research Background — steps 44-45 (pages 51-52)

1. It should be between 1000 and 1500 words.
2. Reviewing what other researchers have done on a particular topic.
3. A good literature review is not just a summary of relevant past research. It involves critically evaluating, reorganizing, and synthesizing the work of others.
4. Discovering inconsistencies and contradictions to form the basis of the research.
5. Explain the material from beginning to end.

Page 52:
6. Keep sentences short.
7. Avoid sentences that contain the word "and."
8. Reviewing a poorly written article. *(the slide gives only this fragment; the checker keeps it as a manual rule and does not interpret it)*
9. Have other people read the article.

*(skill note)* The approved sources from the search phase (Concept, Challenge, Application, Methodology) are the raw material for this section.

## 7. Methodology — steps 46-48 (pages 53-55)

Page 53:
1. It should be between 500 and 1000 words.
2. Explain the research steps.
3. Justify the steps of the research method.
4. Explain the appropriateness of the chosen method.
5. Name articles to justify your choice.

Page 54, "Statistical population":
6. The statistical population in question should be fully described.
7. Slide examples (Persian): تکواندوکاران ملی پوش البرزی · بیماران دیابتی استان تهران · دانشجویان گیمر دانشکده هوش مصنوعی دانشگاه آزاد اسلامی واحد کرج
8. If we have several statistical populations, all of them should be stated.

Page 55:
9. The description of the statistical population should be in the form of a regular paragraph, not in the form of a list.
10. Introduce the data collection tool, such as: questionnaire, interview, or library.
11. State the time during which the data was collected.
12. List the variables that were measured.

## 8. Findings — steps 49-50 (pages 56-57)

Page 56:
1. It should be between 1000 and 1500 words.
2. In this section, the research findings are discussed.
3. The results are presented with full explanations.
4. Use tables to display your findings.
5. Use three to five tables in this section.

Page 57:
6. Include one to two figures in this section.
7. Information presented in tables or figures must always be summarized and explained in the text.
8. There must always be clear references to tables and figures in the text, and these references should appear before the tables or figures themselves.
9. Use charts within the findings section as well.

## 9. Conclusion — steps 51-52 (pages 58-59)

Page 58:
1. It should be between 1000 and 1500 words.
2. The conclusion is the most important part of the article.
3. In this part, we do not include any figures, tables, or in-text references.

Page 59, in this section:
4. The main objective of the article
5. The significance of the research
6. The relevant results,
7. the findings
8. The main limitation of the study should be fully stated.
