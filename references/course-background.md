# Course background (slides, pages 1-14)

Source: the slide deck "Research method and presentation" by Niloofar Mirzaei Chahardeh, Ph.D Candidate, Islamic Azad University (I.A.U.). The slides' side banner reads "Data Base". Contents slide: Chapter 1: Method of Search · Chapter 2: Writing an Article.

Reference (page 3): «شیوه ارائه مطالب علمی و فنی»، دکتر سید محمد تقی روحانی رانکوهی، انتشارات جلوه.

Chapter 1 opens (page 5) with four topics: Research Concept · Types of Research · Prerequisites Research · Steps in conducting research.

## Research concept (page 6)

Research is a form of exploration and inquiry used to understand and solve problems, and is necessary for finding solutions.

## Types of research (page 7)

- Customize Research: performed to demand of an institution or business.
- Obligatory Research: like a master's degree thesis or a doctoral dissertation.

*(skill)* Ask which type applies. Obligatory research is the case §3 of `validation.md` grades: an undergraduate thesis is rejected at S4 and a doctoral dissertation is what the tier rubric rewards, so the answer changes which theses the user may cite.

## Prerequisites research (page 8)

- Dominate the English Language
- Dominate the AI Tools
- High Search Speed
- Dominate your Thoughts and Goals

*(skill)* How the skill supports each one: English: most sources are English, so write search queries in English and offer to explain passages. AI tools: use search and fetch tools to screen candidates quickly. Search speed: run the keyword searches in parallel. Thoughts and goals: if the user's goal is unclear, settle it first (Phase A in `SKILL.md`).

## Steps in conducting research (page 9)

1. Identify the area
2. Identify the subject
3. Identify Reference

### Identify the area (page 10)

Examples: Software, Formal Method, Network, Data Science, Data Mining, Security …

### Identify the subject (page 11)

Goals + Ability + Time + Personality Attribute.

### Identify reference (page 12)

| Identify | Question |
|---|---|
| Conceptology | What |
| Challenges | Why |
| Applications | Why |
| Methodology | How |

The slide groups Challenges and Applications under one "Why" and draws a What / Why / How triangle. *(skill)* Read Challenges as "why it is hard" and Applications as "why it matters".

## Keywords for search (page 13)

Formula: **This Word + Your Research Keyword**.

Tutorial · Survey · Overview · An Introduction · Bibliography · References · Archive · Method · Technique · Algorithm · Model · Concept · Thesis · Taxonomy · Approach · Books

## Validation (page 14)

Four kinds: Validation of Conference Papers · Validation of Journal Articles · Validation of Thesis · Validation of Books. Details are in `validation.md`; ranking and the four reading categories are in `validation.md` (section 5) and `reading.md`.
