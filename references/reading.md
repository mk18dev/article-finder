# Reading and extraction rules per category (step S11)

Source: slides pages 33-38 ("Categories for Reference Ranking" and the four category slides). Each approved source goes into exactly one primary category, and the reading depth depends on that category.

The four categories: 1 Conceptual Analysis · 2 Challenge Identification · 3 Application Review · 4 Methodological Review.

## 1. Conceptual Analysis (answers "What")

Slide rules:
1. Focus on the abstract, introduction, and conclusion of articles
2. Concentrate on the first and second chapters of books and theses
3. Extract key sentences related to fundamental definitions
4. Ensure comprehensive coverage of all definitions and perspectives
5. Identify the similarities and differences between various perspectives

## 2. Challenge Identification (answers "Why is it hard")

Slide rules:
1. Focus on the abstract, introduction, and conclusion of articles
2. Concentrate on the first and second chapters of books and theses
3. Extract key foundational sentences related to general challenges
4. Understanding the overall complexities of the issue
5. Categorizing quantitative challenges based on their nature

Signal phrases on the next slide: "The Problem is" · "The Main Challenge of" · "The Main Problems of".

## 3. Application Review (answers "Why does it matter")

Slide rules:
1. Focus on the abstract, introduction, and conclusion of articles
2. Concentrate on the first and second chapters of books and theses
3. Extract key foundational keywords related to the applications of the topic
4. Gain a thorough understanding of the relevant application areas of the issue
5. Precisely determine the position of the issue within various application domains

## 4. Methodological Review (answers "How")

Slide rules:
1. Thorough study of all sections of the articles
2. Thorough study of all chapters of the thesis
3. Thorough study of all relevant chapters of the book
4. Identification of the main ideas of existing methods

## Recording the result *(skill)*

For the report, write one extract per approved source: the single most useful point in its category, in your own words (a short quoted phrase at most). If you could only access the abstract, say so, and do not tick S11 for a Methodology source: that category needs the full text, so mark it ❓.

For Conceptual, Challenge and Application sources, the abstract + introduction + conclusion (or chapters 1-2 of a book or thesis) are enough to tick S11, because that is all the slides ask you to read.
